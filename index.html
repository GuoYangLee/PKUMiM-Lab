01 Our research

Mechanics opens
a new view of life.

From the physics of soft matter to new imaging instruments, we connect fundamental mechanics with questions in biology and medicine.

01 / UNDERSTANDWave physics

Wave mechanics of
soft living matter

How do stress, anisotropy, and viscoelasticity shape wave propagation? We investigate guided and surface waves to reveal the mechanical states of soft materials and tissues.

  • Acoustoelasticity
  • Surface waves
  • Active matter
02 / IMAGEImaging & inversion

Seeing tissue mechanics
across scales

We develop optical and ultrasound shear wave imaging, combining precise wave measurements with mechanical models and physics-informed inversion to map tissue properties.

  • Optical elastography
  • Ultrasound
  • Full-wave inversion
03 / MODULATEMechanobiology

Ultrasound as a
mechanical input

We study how ultrasound-generated forces couple to cells and tissues, connecting acoustic radiation force, mechanosensation, and neuromodulation.

  • Focused ultrasound
  • Mechanosensation
  • Brain modulation
04 / CREATEAcoustic fabrication

Shaping materials
with sound

We explore ultrasound-driven material formation and sensing, developing approaches to 3D and 4D printing for creating and controlling soft structures.

  • Ultrasound printing
  • Hydrogels
  • 3D / 4D fabrication
